# Vision

Governed Content is a working name for a Drupal module focused on selected content that should follow the lifecycle of a website.

The module is not intended to synchronize all editorial content.

It focuses on content such as:

- legal notice pages;
- privacy policy;
- terms of use;
- contact page;
- corporate pages;
- compliance-related FAQ pages.

The goal is to allow site builders and agencies to manage selected content as versionable, reviewable and deployable content, following a controlled Dev  Staging  Production workflow.
